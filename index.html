FERM

The FERM is the official framework for operational monitoring global progress and disseminating good practices for the UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration.

It also supports countries in reporting areas under restoration for the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework Target 2, which aims to restore 30% of degraded ecosystems by 2030.

FERM is for everyone involved in restoration, including national and local governments, NGOs and civil society, Indigenous Peoples, local communities, researchers and academia.
